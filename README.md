# Ship King

i don't wait for the tool i want. i build it, ship it, and move on to the next idea that won't leave me alone.

if you only open one repo, open BonaMind. i built it for myself first.

## projects

**[BonaMind](https://github.com/shipking-ai/BonaMind)**
also at [bonamind.app](https://bonamind.app)
i was forgetting everything i studied. so i built the study app i wished existed. paste anything in, get flashcards, lessons, and quizzes, and FSRS schedules the reviews so it sticks. web, PWA, and Android.

**[The Red Ring](https://github.com/shipking-ai/global-news)**
also at [theredring.vercel.app](https://theredring.vercel.app)
normal news feeds felt dead. so i built one with a globe you can spin, live TV, a ticker, and an archive back to 1789.

**[SlangVault](https://github.com/shipking-ai/slangvault)**
also at [slangvault-ten.vercel.app](https://slangvault-ten.vercel.app)
slang moves faster than dictionaries. SlangVault tries to keep up. 211 entries, 69 meme formats, and the reaction GIFs people actually use.

**[Nightfall](https://github.com/shipking-ai/nightfall)**
a rainy, lamp-lit city at 3:17am. free-roam, multiplayer, entirely procedural, runs in your browser with zero downloads. walk around and see what happens.

**[OmniHarness](https://github.com/shipking-ai/omniharness)**
local-first agent execution in Go. deterministic task analysis, multi-agent scheduling, policy-gated tools. built for agents that follow the rules you set, not ones that freestyle.
